Route 01 · 5 places

The “8D Chongqing” Route

Kuixinglou + the train-through-building — your two must-sees together3–4 hours · Metro Line 2 + some uphill walking
⌄

Metro stations for this route

L2 · Linjiangmen 临江门 L2 · Liziba 李子坝 L2 · Fotuguan 佛图关 L1 · Eling 鹅岭

Start at Linjiangmen on Line 2. Ride directly to Liziba, continue one stop to Fotuguan, then walk to Eling Park and TESTBED 2. The hillside sections have steps and slopes.

01

Kuixinglou Sky Plaza

魁星楼广场

METRO Line 2 · Linjiangmen (临江门)

1 2

A remarkable “ground-level” square connected to a skybridge about 22 floors above the streets below. A perfect example of Chongqing’s unusual vertical city.

02

Liziba Monorail Viewing Platform

李子坝单轨穿楼观景平台

METRO Line 2 · Liziba (李子坝)

1 2

Watch trains pass directly through the middle of a high-rise building. The public viewing area is next to Liziba Station.

03

Fotuguan Park

佛图关公园

METRO Line 2 · Fotuguan (佛图关)

1 2

A green hillside area with winding paths and glimpses of the city and railway. A natural stop on the way up toward Eling.

04

Eling Park

鹅岭公园

METRO Line 1 · Eling (鹅岭)

1 2

A peaceful park on a high ridge with impressive views toward the rivers and central Chongqing. Expect a little climbing.

05

TESTBED 2 (Eling Second Factory)

鹅岭二厂 / 贰厂文创公园

METRO Line 1 · Eling (鹅岭)

1 2

A former printing-factory complex turned into a small arts and café district. Great for industrial architecture and photographs.

Route 02 · 2 places

The Ring: An Indoor Rainforest

A shopping mall with a spectacular forest and waterfall1.5–3 hours · Indoor-friendly
⌄

Metro stations for this route

L5 · Chongguang 重光

From Daqingcun (Line 4), change to Line 5 at Dashiba and get off at Chongguang (Exit 1). Both stops below are inside the same mall. Muguang Forest is free, but staff may ask you to scan a WeChat QR code to enter.

01

The Ring Shopping Park

重庆光环购物公园

METRO Line 5 · Chongguang (重光)

12

Much more than a shopping centre: its dramatic glass-roofed interior surrounds a multi-level indoor jungle. There are plenty of restaurants and cafés if you want a relaxed, weatherproof outing.

02

Muguang Forest

沐光森林

METRO Line 5 · Chongguang (重光)

12

Explore a vast indoor botanical garden with elevated walkways, tropical plants and a tall waterfall. Different floors offer completely different views of the greenery.

Route 03 · 3 places

Three Gorges Museum & Great Hall

A museum and a magnificent auditorium facing the same square2.5–4 hours · Indoor-friendly
⌄

Metro stations for this route

L2 / 10 · Zengjiayan 曾家岩 L10 · Great Hall 大礼堂

From Daqingcun, use Line 4 → Huahuiyuan (Line 6) → Hongtudi (Line 10) → Zengjiayan. The museum, People’s Square and Great Hall are within a short walk. Start with the museum; it normally closes at 17:00 and is closed on Mondays.

01

Chongqing China Three Gorges Museum

重庆中国三峡博物馆

METRO Line 2 / 10 · Zengjiayan (曾家岩)

12

An excellent indoor museum on local history, the Three Gorges, the river and Chongqing during World War II. General admission is free; take your passport and visit before the afternoon closing time.

02

People’s Square

人民广场

METRO Line 10 · Great Hall (大礼堂)

12

A short open-air crossing connects the museum with the grand auditorium opposite. This is the classic spot for a photograph of the Great Hall.

03

Chongqing People’s Great Hall

重庆市人民大礼堂

METRO Line 10 · Great Hall (大礼堂)

12

An impressive Chinese-style civic building with a huge domed interior. You can often visit the auditorium with a ticket, unless it is being used for an event.

Route 04 · 5 places

Downtown & Night Views

The central square, old-style cliff buildings and the river bridge3–4 hours · Best from late afternoon to night
⌄

Metro stations for this route

L2 · Linjiangmen 临江门 L1 / 6 · Xiaoshizi 小什字 L6 · Grand Theatre 大剧院

Walk from Jiefangbei to Kuixinglou and Hongyadong. Continue toward Qiansimen Bridge if pedestrian access is open; finish across the river near Grand Theatre Station.

01

Jiefangbei Pedestrian Street

解放碑步行街

METRO Line 2 · Linjiangmen (临江门)

1 2

The busy heart of downtown Chongqing, full of towers, shops and local food. The Liberation Monument stands in the central square.

02

Kuixinglou Sky Plaza

魁星楼

METRO Line 2 · Linjiangmen (临江门)

1 2

See how a square at street level can also be more than twenty floors above another road. One of the city’s most unusual photo spots.

03

Hongyadong (Hongya Cave)

洪崖洞

METRO Line 1 / 6 · Xiaoshizi (小什字)

1 2

A dramatic collection of traditional-style buildings built into a riverside cliff. The illuminated exterior is especially photogenic after dark.

04

Qiansimen Bridge

千厮门大桥

METRO Line 6 · Grand Theatre (大剧院)

1 2

A bridge over the Jialing River, with striking views of Hongyadong and the skyline. Check on-site signs for current pedestrian access.

05

Chongqing Grand Theatre Riverside

重庆大剧院 / 江北嘴江滩公园

METRO Line 6 · Grand Theatre (大剧院)

1 2

Look back across the river for an excellent panorama of Hongyadong and the bridge. One of the best places for evening city photography.

Route 05 · 3 places

South Bank Old Streets

Two atmospheric historic-style neighborhoods across one road2–3 hours · Easy to combine on foot, with stairs
⌄

Metro stations for this route

L6 / Loop · Shangxinjie 上新街

Use Shangxinjie Station (Line 6 or Loop Line). Longmenhao and Xiahaoli are almost opposite each other; follow the hillside streets toward the river.

01

Longmenhao Old Street

龙门浩老街

METRO Line 6 / Loop · Shangxinjie (上新街)

1 2

Restored historic lanes, small courtyards, cafés and views across the Yangtze. A beautiful place to wander without hurrying.

02

Xiahaoli Old Street

下浩里老街

METRO Line 6 / Loop · Shangxinjie (上新街)

1 2

A hillside maze of steps, older buildings and cosy cafés. It is very close to Longmenhao and especially atmospheric near sunset.

03

Nanbin Road Riverside (near Longmenhao)

南滨路

METRO Line 6 / Loop · Shangxinjie (上新街)

1 2

Walk beside the Yangtze and look back at the steep skyline of central Chongqing. This is a relaxing end to the old-street route.

Route 06 · 3 places

Ciqikou Ancient Town

Old streets, tea, snacks and a temple in one area2–3 hours · Best in the morning
⌄

Metro stations for this route

L1 · Ciqikou 磁器口

All three places are in or beside Ciqikou Old Town. Take Line 1 to Ciqikou and walk through the lanes.

01

Ciqikou Ancient Town

磁器口古镇

METRO Line 1 · Ciqikou (磁器口)

1 2

Stone-paved alleys, old-style shops, teahouses and street food in a former riverside trading town. Venture into the side streets when the main lane gets crowded.

02

Baolun Temple

宝轮寺

METRO Line 1 · Ciqikou (磁器口)

1 2

A historic Buddhist temple tucked into the old town. A calmer place to visit between the lively shopping streets.

03

Ciqikou Old Wharf

磁器口码头

METRO Line 1 · Ciqikou (磁器口)

1 2

Continue toward the Jialing River and the old port area. It is a pleasant place to finish your walk through Ciqikou.

Route 07 · 3 places

Guanyinqiao: Food & City Lights

A compact evening walk in Jiangbei District1–2 hours · Best in the evening
⌄

Metro stations for this route

L3 / 9 · Guanyinqiao 观音桥

Everything is around Guanyinqiao Station (Lines 3 and 9). No additional metro ride is needed once you arrive.

01

Guanyinqiao Pedestrian Street

观音桥步行街

METRO Line 3 / 9 · Guanyinqiao (观音桥)

1 2

One of Chongqing’s liveliest shopping districts, surrounded by tall buildings and giant screens. Great for seeing the modern city after dark.

02

Beicheng Tianjie

北城天街

METRO Line 3 / 9 · Guanyinqiao (观音桥)

1 2

A large shopping and dining district within an easy walk of the main plaza. Useful for a meal, coffee or shopping break.

03

Guanyinqiao Food Street

观音桥好吃街

METRO Line 3 / 9 · Guanyinqiao (观音桥)

1 2

A busy street-food area where you can sample Chongqing snacks. Many dishes are spicy, so ask for mild if needed.

Route 08 · 2 places

At the Meeting of Two Rivers

Raffles City and the tip of the peninsula1–1½ hours · Easy short walk
⌄

Metro stations for this route

L1 · Chaotianmen 朝天门

Line 1 to Chaotianmen (朝天门). The two stops are right beside one another: explore the riverfront and the Raffles City complex.

01

Chaotianmen Riverfront

朝天门广场

METRO Line 1 · Chaotianmen (朝天门)

1 2

Stand near the meeting point of the Yangtze and Jialing rivers. Watch the boats and enjoy the skyline at the very tip of the peninsula.

02

Raffles City Chongqing

重庆来福士

METRO Line 1 · Chaotianmen (朝天门)

1 2

A spectacular complex of towers connected by a horizontal skybridge. Even from the outside, it is one of Chongqing’s most striking modern landmarks.
