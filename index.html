Pocket travel notes

Chongqing · Simple Tourist Guide

Six short sightseeing routes, arranged by nearby places. No apps, checkboxes or sign-in.

Your starting metro: Line 4 · Daqingcun (大庆村). Use the metro to reach each route.

1. The “8D Chongqing” Route 2. Downtown & Night Views 3. South Bank Old Streets 4. Ciqikou Ancient Town 5. Near the University: Parks 6. Guanyinqiao: Food & City Lights
ROUTE 01

The “8D Chongqing” Route

Kuixinglou + the train-through-building — your two must-sees together

3–4 hours · Metro Line 2 + some uphill walking

Route: Start at Linjiangmen on Line 2. Ride directly to Liziba, continue one stop to Fotuguan, then walk to Eling Park and TESTBED 2. The hillside sections have steps and slopes.

01Kuixinglou Sky Plaza 魁星楼

Metro: Line 2 · Linjiangmen (临江门)

↗ View photos

A remarkable “ground-level” square connected to a skybridge about 22 floors above the streets below. A perfect example of Chongqing’s unusual vertical city.

02Liziba Monorail Viewing Platform 李子坝单轨穿楼观景平台

Metro: Line 2 · Liziba (李子坝)

↗ View photos

Watch trains pass directly through the middle of a high-rise building. The public viewing area is next to Liziba Station.

03Fotuguan Park 佛图关公园

Metro: Line 2 · Fotuguan (佛图关)

↗ View photos

A green hillside area with winding paths and glimpses of the city and railway. A natural stop on the way up toward Eling.

04Eling Park 鹅岭公园

Metro: Line 1 · Eling (鹅岭)

↗ View photos

A peaceful park on a high ridge with impressive views toward the rivers and central Chongqing. Expect a little climbing.

05TESTBED 2 (Eling Second Factory) 鹅岭二厂 / 贰厂文创公园

Metro: Line 1 · Eling (鹅岭)

↗ View photos

A former printing-factory complex turned into a small arts and café district. Great for industrial architecture and photographs.

ROUTE 02

Downtown & Night Views

The central square, old-style cliff buildings and the river bridge

3–4 hours · Best from late afternoon to night

Route: Walk from Jiefangbei to Kuixinglou and Hongyadong. Continue toward Qiansimen Bridge if pedestrian access is open; finish across the river near Grand Theatre Station.

01Jiefangbei Pedestrian Street 解放碑步行街

Metro: Line 2 · Linjiangmen (临江门)

↗ View photos

The busy heart of downtown Chongqing, full of towers, shops and local food. The Liberation Monument stands in the central square.

02Kuixinglou Sky Plaza 魁星楼

Metro: Line 2 · Linjiangmen (临江门)

↗ View photos

See how a square at street level can also be more than twenty floors above another road. One of the city’s most unusual photo spots.

03Hongyadong (Hongya Cave) 洪崖洞

Metro: Line 1 / 6 · Xiaoshizi (小什字)

↗ View photos

A dramatic collection of traditional-style buildings built into a riverside cliff. The illuminated exterior is especially photogenic after dark.

04Qiansimen Bridge 千厮门大桥

Metro: Line 6 · Grand Theatre (大剧院)

↗ View photos

A bridge over the Jialing River, with striking views of Hongyadong and the skyline. Check on-site signs for current pedestrian access.

05Chongqing Grand Theatre Riverside 重庆大剧院 / 江北嘴江滩公园

Metro: Line 6 · Grand Theatre (大剧院)

↗ View photos

Look back across the river for an excellent panorama of Hongyadong and the bridge. One of the best places for evening city photography.

ROUTE 03

South Bank Old Streets

Two atmospheric historic-style neighborhoods across one road

2–3 hours · Easy to combine on foot, with stairs

Route: Use Shangxinjie Station (Line 6 or Loop Line). Longmenhao and Xiahaoli are almost opposite each other; follow the hillside streets toward the river.

01Longmenhao Old Street 龙门浩老街

Metro: Line 6 / Loop · Shangxinjie (上新街)

↗ View photos

Restored historic lanes, small courtyards, cafés and views across the Yangtze. A beautiful place to wander without hurrying.

02Xiahaoli Old Street 下浩里老街

Metro: Line 6 / Loop · Shangxinjie (上新街)

↗ View photos

A hillside maze of steps, older buildings and cosy cafés. It is very close to Longmenhao and especially atmospheric near sunset.

03Nanbin Road Riverside (near Longmenhao) 南滨路

Metro: Line 6 / Loop · Shangxinjie (上新街)

↗ View photos

Walk beside the Yangtze and look back at the steep skyline of central Chongqing. This is a relaxing end to the old-street route.

ROUTE 04

Ciqikou Ancient Town

Old streets, tea, snacks and a temple in one area

2–3 hours · Best in the morning

Route: All three places are in or beside Ciqikou Old Town. Take Line 1 to Ciqikou and walk through the lanes.

01Ciqikou Ancient Town 磁器口古镇

Metro: Line 1 · Ciqikou (磁器口)

↗ View photos

Stone-paved alleys, old-style shops, teahouses and street food in a former riverside trading town. Venture into the side streets when the main lane gets crowded.

02Baolun Temple 宝轮寺

Metro: Line 1 · Ciqikou (磁器口)

↗ View photos

A historic Buddhist temple tucked into the old town. A calmer place to visit between the lively shopping streets.

03Ciqikou Old Wharf 磁器口码头

Metro: Line 1 · Ciqikou (磁器口)

↗ View photos

Continue toward the Jialing River and the old port area. It is a pleasant place to finish your walk through Ciqikou.

ROUTE 05

Near the University: Parks

A green walk close to your Daqingcun starting point

2–3 hours · Hills and park paths

Route: Start at Dashiba (Line 4 or 5) and head to Hong’en Temple Park. Walk through the park, then continue toward Flower Garden and Huahuiyuan Station (Line 4 or 6).

01Hong'en Temple Forest Park 鸿恩寺森林公园

Metro: Line 4 / 5 · Dashiba (大石坝)

↗ View photos

A large hillside park with trees, viewpoints and winding paths. A good short trip from the university area.

02Hong'en Pavilion 鸿恩阁

Metro: Line 4 / 5 · Dashiba (大石坝)

↗ View photos

An elegant Chinese-style pavilion rising above the park. Its rooflines and city views make it a memorable photo stop.

03Chongqing Flower Garden 重庆花卉园

Metro: Line 4 / 6 · Huahuiyuan (花卉园)

↗ View photos

A pleasant garden and green space near Huahuiyuan Metro Station. Pair it with Hong’en Park for a relaxed outdoor walk.

ROUTE 06

Guanyinqiao: Food & City Lights

A compact evening walk in Jiangbei District

1–2 hours · Best in the evening

Route: Everything is around Guanyinqiao Station (Lines 3 and 9). No additional metro ride is needed once you arrive.

01Guanyinqiao Pedestrian Street 观音桥步行街

Metro: Line 3 / 9 · Guanyinqiao (观音桥)

↗ View photos

One of Chongqing’s liveliest shopping districts, surrounded by tall buildings and giant screens. Great for seeing the modern city after dark.

02Beicheng Tianjie 北城天街

Metro: Line 3 / 9 · Guanyinqiao (观音桥)

↗ View photos

A large shopping and dining district within an easy walk of the main plaza. Useful for a meal, coffee or shopping break.

03Guanyinqiao Food Street 观音桥好吃街

Metro: Line 3 / 9 · Guanyinqiao (观音桥)

↗ View photos

A busy street-food area where you can sample Chongqing snacks. Many dishes are spicy, so ask for mild if needed.

Note: "Metro" means a convenient station for that place, not a door-to-door route. Chongqing has steep streets and different street levels. Check current signs, pedestrian access and directions in Amap (高德地图).
